Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about cryptocurrency and blockchain.

What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a type of digital asset that uses cryptographic techniques and may operate on a blockchain network.

What is a crypto wallet?

A crypto wallet helps users manage the keys used to access their digital assets.

Is cryptocurrency risk-free?

No. Cryptocurrency prices can be volatile, and users can face financial, security and regulatory risks.

What is blockchain technology?

A blockchain is a type of digital ledger that records transactions across a network according to its rules.
